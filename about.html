HLSA Scout — Read Me

HLSA Scout finds local, realistic business opportunities for HLSA's Trusted Referral Partners and NT Partnership — new practices opening, agencies starting up, facilities breaking ground, physicians arriving, practices changing hands, grants landing — across San Antonio, the counties around Bexar, the I-35 Corridor and the small towns of the Hill Country and rural South Texas.

Version 1.3.1 · October 31, 2026

Why it exists. The business press mostly covers the biggest, highest-dollar companies. The opportunities our partners can actually win are often smaller: a 15-doctor family practice opening next month in New Braunfels that needs its physicians credentialed and nurses hired; a new home health agency in Seguin; a hospice starting in a senior community; a rural clinic that just got a grant. HLSA Scout looks specifically for those "boutique" and Mom-and-Pop opportunities — without excluding the big systems — and it does it using only free, public sources. No subscriptions.

🆕 Newly registered healthcare organizations (the hidden gem)

Every clinic, practice, agency or facility that bills insurance must get a federal NPI number — usually weeks before it opens, and often without any news coverage at all. HLSA Scout checks the free CMS NPI Registry for organizations registered in the territory in the last 90 days and lists each with its type, practice address, phone and fax, and the person who registered it — the "authorized official," usually the owner or administrator — with their title and phone number. (The registry is public federal data; it does not include email addresses.) A NEW badge marks ones that appeared since your previous scan.

Keep in mind: an NPI is issued when an organization applies. Some are brand-new businesses, some are existing practices registering a new location or legal entity, and a few never open. Medical-supply, pharmacy and lab registrations are hidden by default (that group is full of tiny supply LLCs).

Where the information comes from

  • Google News search, town by town — this is how HLSA Scout reaches the little local papers, community websites, local TV, blogs and press releases (Herald-Zeitung, Seguin Gazette, Boerne Star, Uvalde Leader-News, Community Impact, KSAT, San Antonio Report and many more). Every smaller town gets its own health-business search; the big cities get one search per category.
  • Direct feeds from local news outlets and the home health, hospice, senior-living and hospital trade press (Home Health Care News, Hospice News, Senior Housing News, Becker's) — trade-press stories are only kept when they name a place in the territory.
  • The CMS NPI Registry (public federal data) for newly registered organizations.
Subscription sites. For outlets like the Express-News, the Business Journals or the Statesman, HLSA Scout only ever shows the publisher's own public headline — it never gets around a paywall. Instead, those stories carry a 🔓 Free versions button that looks for the same story at outlets without a hard paywall (other papers, TV stations, press releases), plus a one-click Google News search with the paywalled sites excluded.

How to use it

  1. Start it. Double-click HLSAScout.exe. A small window opens. (Windows may ask once whether to allow it — it only talks to this computer and to the public news/registry sites.)
  2. Click Scan Now. In about a minute your browser opens the Scan Report. Scan whenever you like — daily or weekly works well.
  3. Browse the tabs:
    • 📊 Scan Report — Top Opportunities, the newest registered organizations, and the best items in each category.
    • 📰 Opportunity Wire — every story, with filters for category, area, partner ("show me what fits Wave Online"), a search box, and a "Hide big systems" switch.
    • 🆕 New Organizations — the full NPI list with type, area, partner and "only new since my last scan" filters.
    • ⭐ Watchlist — what you've saved, tracked as leads.
  4. Save a lead: tick the small box in the bottom-right corner of any story or organization. On the Watchlist, give it a status (New → Researching → Contacted → Meeting set → Pursuing → Won / Not a fit) and notes — both save automatically.
  5. Share: the 📤 button sends a story or organization (with "could be an opportunity for…") through email, text or Teams, or copies it to paste anywhere. On the Watchlist, Share Watchlist as Report downloads a self-contained page you can email to the group, and Download for Excel gives you a spreadsheet.

📈 Market & Plan (laptop only)

A market analysis and business plan for NT Partnership, kept up to date from free public data and from HLSA Scout's own scan. It lives only in the laptop app — never on the public phone edition — because the plan is NT Partnership's.

  • Market at a glance — Medicare home health agencies and hospices in the territory, nursing homes and how many contract (agency) nursing hours they buy, clinicians who bill Medicare, new organizations from the NPI Registry, and workforce size and pay from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
  • Market share distribution — which owners hold the area's Medicare home health and hospice patients (chains vs. owner-operated agencies, with a concentration score), contract labor by county and the most contract-reliant facilities, clinicians by specialty, and new-practice formation. Staffing and credentialing firms don't publish their revenue, so share is measured on the customer side.
  • Niche opportunities — ranked, each with its evidence and named starting targets.
  • Competitive landscape — a researched list of local and national staffing and credentialing competitors, with HLSA partners (Nurses Etc, Wave Online) positioned as collaborators.
  • Business plan — twelve sections with live numbers, including the HLSA Consulting Collective (ad hoc teams of HLSA members and partners for strategic planning, financial analysis and more) and a recommended, compliance-minded structure for the HLSA relationship. [Highlighted] items are for NT Partnership to fill in.
  • Financial model — a 3-year projection whose assumptions you can change on the page; it recalculates instantly and remembers your numbers on that computer. Download business plan saves the whole plan as a file Word opens.

Market data refreshes automatically once a week during a scan (the government sources update quarterly or yearly), or any time with ⟳ Refresh market data.

📱 The phone edition

HLSA Scout also runs on any phone or tablet — no app store, no install, no laptop needed: daneathery-cloud.github.io/hlsa-scout (there is also a 📱 Phone version link at the top of every page in the laptop app).

  • It refreshes itself twice a day (about 7 am and 4 pm Central) — the same scan the laptop app runs, done on GitHub's servers. The time of the scan you're looking at is shown at the top; tap ⟳ Refresh to load the newest one. If a newer scan arrives while the page is open, a banner offers to load it.
  • Same tabs, same filters, same buttons — Share, 🔓 Free versions, and the Watchlist with status and notes. Organizations are marked NEW for the first week after they appear.
  • Your phone keeps its own watchlist, separate from the laptop app and from other people's phones. Share Watchlist as Report and Excel (CSV) work from the phone too.
  • Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app — iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen. On iPhones this also keeps your watchlist from being cleared after a few weeks of not opening it.
  • The link is public but unlisted (search engines are asked not to index it). Everything on it is already-public news and federal registry data; nothing about you or your watchlist is ever uploaded.

Reading a story card

  • 📍 the town or area · the category · Big system (a large health system or chain — still shown, just labeled) · Subscription site (use 🔓 Free versions)
  • Could help: the partners it may matter to (NT Partnership in red)
  • Also covered by: other outlets that ran the same story

Good practice

  • Treat everything as a lead to verify, not a fact — call, visit the website, check the NPI record.
  • Coordinate through HLSA before several partners approach the same new practice, so prospects meet one friendly network instead of ten separate pitches.
  • Partner suggestions are informational. Any referral arrangement between partners should follow the usual healthcare rules (no payments for patient referrals; written agreements at fair market value).

Privacy & where your data lives

HLSA Scout runs entirely on your own computer. It does not have accounts, does not upload your watchlist anywhere, and the report pages are served only to your own browser. Your watchlist, statuses and notes are stored in your Windows profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\HLSAScout), so replacing the program with a newer version keeps them. Each partner who runs HLSA Scout has their own separate watchlist — use the Share buttons to pass things along.

Versions & feedback

The version number is shown in the window title, the top-right of every page and here. Changes are listed in CHANGELOG.md. If something looks wrong, click Copy Diagnostics in the HLSA Scout window and send it along with your feedback — suggestions for towns, outlets, search words or partners to add are very welcome; they are easy to tune.
